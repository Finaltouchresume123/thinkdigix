ThinkDigix ("ThinkDigix," "we," "us," or "our") provides digital marketing services including SEO, paid advertising, social media marketing, and web development. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website and consultation process, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using this website or submitting a consultation request, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — when you submit our consultation form, we collect your full name, company name, email address, phone number, selected service of interest, and any details you share about your business or goals.
  • Information collected automatically — when you browse our website, we (and our third-party tools) may automatically collect your IP address, browser type, device type, referring pages, and the pages you visit, through cookies and similar tracking technologies.
  • Communications — if you contact us by email or phone, we keep a record of that correspondence to respond to your enquiry and maintain a history of our discussions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to consultation requests and follow up with proposals or quotes
  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • Measure and improve the performance of our own marketing campaigns
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraudulent or unauthorised activity
  • Communicate updates relevant to services you have enquired about, where you have not opted out

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand site traffic, and measure advertising performance. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect how parts of the site function. Where required by applicable law, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted third-party services to operate this website and our marketing activity, including:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers consultation form submissions to our team.
  • Google Analytics / Google Ads — helps us understand site traffic and measure advertising performance.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel — may be used to measure and optimise social advertising, where applicable.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy, and information submitted through our forms or collected via tracking tools may be transmitted to and stored on their servers, which may be located outside your country of residence.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (such as the tools listed above), or when required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request. We may also disclose information to protect the rights, property, or safety of ThinkDigix, our clients, or others.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including to respond to your enquiry, maintain business records, and comply with legal or accounting obligations. Where information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business use and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at siddharthaacharya2000@gmail.com.
